CORVANTA INDUSTRIES REMOTE WORK AND PAID TIME OFF POLICY

Policy ID: POL-HR-012. Version 2.1. Effective February 1, 2026. Policy owner: People and Culture.

1. Hybrid Work Model
Corvanta operates a hybrid work model. Employees in hybrid roles are expected to work from the office 3 days per week. Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are the company anchor days for in-office collaboration. Team leaders may set additional in-office days for specific events with at least one week of notice.

2. Fully Remote Arrangements
Fully remote arrangements require approval from both the HR Business Partner and a Vice President. Fully remote arrangements are reviewed annually. Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per year with prior approval from their manager and the Global Mobility team.

3. Home Office Support
Eligible hybrid and remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500. Employees also receive a monthly internet allowance of $50. Company equipment such as laptops and monitors must be returned within 10 days of separation.

4. Paid Time Off Accrual
Paid time off accrues based on years of service. Employees with 0 to 2 years of service accrue 15 days of PTO per year. Employees with 3 to 5 years of service accrue 20 days of PTO per year. Employees with 6 or more years of service accrue 25 days of PTO per year. Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next year and must be used by March 31.

5. Sick Leave and Holidays
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year, separate from PTO. Corvanta observes 11 paid company holidays each year.

6. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption of a child.
