# Corvanta Industries Travel and Expense Policy

Policy ID: POL-FIN-004. Version 3.2. Effective January 1, 2026. Policy owner: Corporate Finance.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines how Corvanta employees book business travel and claim reimbursement for business expenses. It applies to all full-time employees, part-time employees and contractors who travel on company business. Managers are accountable for reviewing expenses submitted by their direct reports.

## 2. Air Travel

Employees must book economy class for flights with a scheduled duration under 6 hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from a Senior Vice President before booking. Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance whenever possible to secure lower fares. All flights must be booked through the corporate travel portal, Navan.

## 3. Lodging

Hotel stays are capped at $275 per night in Tier 1 cities, which are New York, San Francisco, London and Tokyo. In all other locations hotel stays are capped at $190 per night. Employees must select hotels from the preferred hotel program when one is available in the destination city. Room upgrades, minibar charges and in-room movies are not reimbursable.

## 4. Meals and Per Diem

The meal per diem is $85 per day for domestic travel and $110 per day for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except during client entertainment that has been pre-approved by a Director. Approved client entertainment is limited to $60 per attendee including alcohol. The meal per diem is reduced by 50 percent on the first and last day of a trip.

## 5. Ground Transportation

Ride-share services are reimbursable at the standard tier only; premium tiers such as Uber Black are not reimbursable. Employees who use a personal vehicle for business are reimbursed for mileage at $0.70 per mile. Rental cars must be compact or intermediate class unless four or more employees are travelling together.

## 6. Receipts and Expense Reports

Itemized receipts are required for any single expense over $25. Expense reports must be submitted in Concur within 30 days of the end of the trip. Expense reports submitted more than 60 days after the end of the trip will not be reimbursed unless the Chief Financial Officer grants a written exception. Reimbursements are paid by direct deposit within 10 business days of manager approval.

## 7. Corporate Card

The Corvanta corporate card must be used for all travel bookings and business meals. Personal use of the corporate card is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action. Cardholders must reconcile their corporate card statement by the 5th business day of each month.

## 8. Approval Thresholds

Total trip expenses above $5,000 require approval from a Vice President before the trip. International trips of any value require approval from the employee's Director. Violations of this policy may result in denial of reimbursement and disciplinary action up to and including termination.
